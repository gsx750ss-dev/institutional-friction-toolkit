Institutional Friction Toolkit

Post-Incident Stabilization ｜ 事後安定化

証拠・権限・状態を保全し、事後レビューを開始できる状態に整えます。

Activation Condition ｜ 起動条件

This document is a conditional reference by default.

It is intended to be populated, referenced, or activated only under one or more of the following conditions:

  • A publicly acknowledged AI-related incident has occurred, or
  • Institutional, regulatory, or review bodies explicitly request post-incident stabilization references.

If none of these conditions are met, this document should remain unused as an operational reference.

No active operation, update, or curation is expected outside the conditions stated above.

Purpose ｜ 目的

This document provides a neutral reference for institutional stabilization after incidents involving recursive or autonomous AI systems.

It does not propose policies, mandates actions, or assign responsibility.

Scope ｜ 範囲

  • Reference-only material
  • No enforcement or recommendations
  • No attribution of fault or intent

Typical Immediate Failures Observed ｜ 典型的に観測される初期失敗

  • Acceleration of competitive deployment
  • Informal precedent formation without review
  • Responsibility diffusion across organizations
  • Review processes bypassed under urgency

Reference-Only Options Commonly Considered ｜ 参照専用の一般的選択肢

  • Temporary suspension of review-based approvals
  • Clarification of decision and responsibility boundaries
  • Cross-organizational information sharing
  • Preservation of audit and review records

Notes ｜ 注記

Use of this material does not constitute precedent for approval, deployment, or policy adoption.

Non-binding public reference. It does not certify, direct, or determine compliance. ｜ 非拘束の公開参照資料です。
