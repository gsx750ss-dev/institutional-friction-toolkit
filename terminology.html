Institutional Friction Toolkit

Terminology

Review definitions and scope.

Usage Condition

This document exists solely to reduce misinterpretation, terminological ambiguity, or semantic drift when such issues are externally observed.

It does not prescribe definitions, enforce usage, or require active management.

If no material confusion or conflicting terminology is observed, no action, update, or reference is required.

No active operation, expansion, or management is expected unless material confusion or conflicting terminology is externally observed.

Purpose

This document exists solely to reduce misinterpretation.

No normative, prescriptive, or directive content is defined here.

Recursive Self-Reconstruction

A system capability in which internal structures, models, or processes are modified or regenerated by the system itself.

Institutional Responsibility

The ability to clearly identify accountable human or organizational actors for decisions, approvals, and outcomes.

Review Rejection

A determination that a system or operation is not eligible for review without implying prohibition or enforcement.

Post-Incident Stabilization

Institutional measures intended to prevent escalation, precedent formation, or responsibility diffusion following an incident.

Non-binding public reference. It does not certify, direct, or determine compliance.
